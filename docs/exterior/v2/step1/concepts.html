Exterior v2 · Step 1: seven concepts as quick elevations

Simple massing on your real building (stilt + 3 flats, corner plot): everything grey except each concept's accent, at dusk with facade lights on. Approval flags use the assumed plot margins (front ≈ 2.5 m, sides ≈ 1.3 m): check with the architect. Grey test: each concept rendered all-grey must differ from every other concept and from the Architect's design.
